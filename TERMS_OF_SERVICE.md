# Terms of Service for Tahan

**Effective Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Platform:** Tahan — Verified Student Housing & Utility Transparency (`https://tahan.cheyngadgets.site`)

---

## 1. Nature of the Platform (Zero-Broker Discovery Directory)

Tahan is an independent student housing discovery and utility transparency platform for students, parents, and property owners. **Tahan is not a real estate broker, rental agency, payment processor, or property manager.** We charge ₱0 in broker commissions or booking fees to both students and landlords.

---

## 2. Utility Rate & Safety Disclosures

1. **Local Grid Baseline Reference:** Tahan displays the prevailing local electric utility baseline rate (such as CAPELCO's ~₱11.42/kWh in Roxas City, MORE Power in Iloilo, Negros Power in Bacolod, or Meralco in Metro Manila) alongside each boarding house's declared and tenant-reported sub-meter rates for budgeting transparency.
2. **BFP Fire Safety & Flood Elevation:** Fire safety badges (FSIC numbers) and street flood elevation notes are based on landlord submissions and student field surveys. Students and parents are strongly encouraged to use the built-in **4-Point On-Site Inspection Checklist** to verify sub-meters, fire exits, and water pressure in person before signing any lease or paying a deposit.

---

## 3. Property Owner Obligations & 30-Day Auto-Expiration

1. **Accurate Declarations:** Property owners listing rooms in the Host Portal agree to provide accurate monthly base rents, sub-meter electricity rates, water fees, and available bedspace counts.
2. **30-Day Listing Lifecycle:** To prevent stale or occupied rooms from misleading relocating students, listings automatically expire after 30 days unless renewed by the property owner.

---

## 4. Student Review Guidelines & Anti-Defamation Policy

1. **Proof of Tenancy:** Student reviews must reflect genuine personal tenancy or on-site inspection experiences at the boarding house.
2. **Constructive Feedback:** Reviews must focus on factual living conditions (e.g., electricity billing, water pressure, flood resilience, gate security, Wi-Fi reliability). Personal attacks, profanity, or fabricated claims against landlords or fellow boarders are strictly prohibited.
3. **Right of Reply:** Every boarding house owner has the right to post an official **Landlord Reply** directly beneath any student review.

---

## 5. Limitation of Liability

All rental agreements, security deposits, and monthly payments are transacted directly between the student/parent and the property owner. Tahan shall not be held liable for lease disputes, property damage, or utility billing discrepancies between tenants and landlords.
