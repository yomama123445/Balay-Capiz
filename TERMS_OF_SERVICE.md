# Terms of Service for BalayCapiz

**Effective Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Platform:** BalayCapiz — Roxas City Student Housing Portal (`ph.edu.hercor.balaycapiz`)

---

## 1. Nature of the Platform (Zero-Broker Discovery Directory)

BalayCapiz is an independent civic-tech housing discovery and transparency tool for students and landlords in Roxas City, Capiz. **BalayCapiz is not a real estate broker, rental agency, payment processor, or property manager.** We charge ₱0 in broker commissions or booking fees to both students and landlords.

---

## 2. Utility Rate & Safety Disclosures

1. **CAPELCO Baseline Reference:** BalayCapiz displays the prevailing Capiz Electric Cooperative (CAPELCO) residential baseline rate (~₱11.42/kWh as of October 2026) alongside each boarding house's declared and student-reported sub-meter rates for educational and budgeting transparency.
2. **BFP Fire Safety & Flood Elevation:** Fire safety badges (FSIC numbers) and barangay flood elevation notes are based on landlord submissions and student field surveys. Students and parents are strongly encouraged to use the built-in **4-Point On-Site Inspection Checklist** to verify sub-meters, fire exits, and water pressure in person before signing any lease or paying a deposit.

---

## 3. Landlord Obligations & 30-Day Auto-Expiration

1. **Accurate Declarations:** Landlords listing properties in Landlord Studio agree to provide accurate monthly base rents, sub-meter electricity rates, water fees, and available bedspace counts.
2. **30-Day Listing Lifecycle:** To prevent stale or occupied rooms from misleading relocating students, listings automatically expire after 30 days unless renewed with one tap by the landlord.

---

## 4. Student Review Guidelines & Anti-Defamation Policy

1. **Proof of Tenancy:** Student reviews must reflect genuine personal tenancy or on-site inspection experiences at the boarding house.
2. **Respectful Civic Discourse:** Reviews must focus on factual living conditions (e.g., electricity billing, water pressure, flood resilience, gate security, Wi-Fi reliability). Personal attacks, profanity, or fabricated claims against landlords or fellow boarders are strictly prohibited.
3. **Right of Reply:** Every boarding house landlord has the right to post an official **Landlord Reply** directly beneath any student review.

---

## 5. Limitation of Liability

All rental agreements, security deposits, and monthly payments are transacted directly between the student/parent and the property owner. BalayCapiz and Hercor College shall not be held liable for lease disputes, property damage, or utility billing discrepancies between tenants and landlords.
